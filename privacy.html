Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Outdoor Education Map Maker is a prototype made by a group of trainee teachers. Students use it during Outdoor Education lessons to draw maps of a real site, and their teachers set tasks and give feedback on those maps. This page explains what the app stores and why.

What we store

WhoWhat is stored
Teachers Email address, and for Google sign-in the basic profile Google shares (name and email). The Classes, Tasks and Reference Maps a teacher creates, and the feedback they write on students' maps.
Students on a class list First name and register number (entered by their teacher), their Class, the maps they draw, which classmates they worked with, and the teacher's feedback on their maps. Students do not sign in with an email address or password.
Guests The name they type and a short Guest Code, plus the maps they draw.
Every device A temporary, anonymous session so the app can save work, and a note of which device is currently drawing on a shared map.

We ask teachers to enter students' first names only, never full names.

What we use it for

  • To let students open their task, save their map and hand it in.
  • To let teachers see their own Classes' work and write feedback.
  • To let students in a group take turns drawing on the same map.

We do not use the information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone except the services that run the app.

Who can see it

  • A teacher sees only the Classes they teach, and those Classes' tasks, maps and feedback.
  • A student sees only their own maps (and their group's), and their teacher's feedback on them.
  • Students' names are shown at sign-in only to someone who has the lesson's Task Code.

Services we use

  • Supabase: stores the app's data and handles sign-in.
  • Netlify: hosts this website.
  • Google: only if a teacher chooses "Sign in with Google", to confirm who they are. We receive their name and email address and nothing else from their Google account.

How long we keep it

Work is kept until a teacher deletes it. Deleting a Class removes its class list and all its students' maps and feedback. Archiving a Class hides it but keeps the work. You can ask us to delete any information at any time using the contact below.
